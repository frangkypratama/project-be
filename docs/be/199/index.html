    Tibu ma jumpang tingki

    BE 199Logu No. 132do = AsBirama 4/4Lagu: Joseph klug 1529

    Memuat not angka dan not balok…

    Ende

    1.Sai ingot Jesus, Tuhanmi, naung ro tu hita jolma.Na tumadingkon surgo i, na gabe dongan jolma.Sai unang halupahon i, ai i do hangoluanmi.Sai puji ma Ibana.

    2.Sai ingot Jesus, Tuhanmi sitaon godang na bernitDi hau na pinarsilang i, humophop ho, parsahit.Ai ho tama manaon sude na mangonai tu Jesus i.Sai puji ma Ibana.

    3.Sai ingot Jesus, Tuhanmi na hehe do IbanaDi ari patoluhon i, rumar do tanomanNa.Ai ala ni tarbahen do mangolu sogot dohot ho.Pasangap ma Ibana.

    4.Sai ingot Jesus, Tuhanmi na sangap do Ibana.Manaek muse tu surgo i tu lambung ni AmaNa.Inganan hasonangan i, rade di ho dibaen disi.Tangihon ma hataNa.

    5.Sai ingot Jesus, Tuhanmi ai mulak do IbanaMuse, manguhum jolma i, tangkas didok hataNa.Pature ma roham tongtong, saleleng ho di tano on.Pasangap ma Tuhanmu.

    6.O Jesus, tatap ma au on, sondangi ma rohangku;Urupi au di tano on, maniru ho, Rajangku.Uhirhon ma tu rohangki, goarMu na badia i;Pamasuk au tu surgo.
