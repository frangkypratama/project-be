    315

    Na salpu do arian i, soluk ma ro na holom i

    BE 315Logu No. 35do = AsBirama 3/4Lagu: Johann Heerman 1647

    Memuat not angka dan not balok…

    Ende

    1.Na salpu do arian i, soluk ma ro na holom i.O Jesus, nang saborngin on sondangi hami on tongtong.

    2.Na jorbut, mara, rugi pe, diambat Ho do i sude:Ipe pinuji goarMi dibahen denggan basaMi.

    3.Pambaenannami sasude, na maralohon Ho hape,Sai sesa ma luhutna i, ai marsak hami ala ni.

    4.Urasi, baen ias muse ma tondi dohot daging pe;Ai naeng sonang saborngin on do rohanami sai tongtong

    5.Sai dokkon ma suruanMi, mangambat jea sasude.Nang saluhut di tano on, na tau mangago hami on.

    Lagu: Johann Heerman 1647
