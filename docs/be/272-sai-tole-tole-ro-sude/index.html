    272

    Sai tole, tole, ro sude.

    BE 272Logu No. 249do = GBirama 4/4Lagu: Hieronymus Annoni 1770

    Memuat not angka dan not balok…

    Ende

    1.Sai tole, tole, ro sude, ihuthon Jesus i.Marhohos hasintongan be di paraloan i.Mander silang i, Partogi Tuhna i,Sarune pe mangkuling do. sai tole, tole ro.

    2.Pangansi do musunta i, sai songon na burju.Hape gok sipaoto i, sitolon na tutu.Diela hita be mambaen na so ture,Mangago daging, tondi pe, dungo, tajaga be,

    3.Solokkon hatigoaranmi baen patahananmi.Nang pe haporseaon i bane lombulombu i.Nang patta sai ringgas, rohanta pe hiras.Mamaritahon dame i, baen halolomo i.

    4.Antong sai paralohon ma haporseaonmi.Dipargogoi Debata ma sipartangiang iTajom podangta i, i ma hataNa i,Maruli tumpal do sude na so sumurut be.

    Lagu: Hieronymus Annoni 1770
