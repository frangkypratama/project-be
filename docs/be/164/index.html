    O Tuhan Jesus, Ho Rajangku

    BE 164Logu No. 107do = ABirama 4/4Lagu: Julius Schröder, 1850

    Memuat not angka dan not balok…

    Ende

    1.O Tuhan Jesus, Ho Rajangku, tangihon au, naposoMon!Naeng topotanku do dosangku tu Ho, Sigomgom tano on.Ai naeng mangolu tondingki marhitehite mudarMi.

    2.Godang do dosa ni tanganku, rohangku pe gok dosa do,Nang mata pe, nang pamatangku, luhutna i gok dosa do.Sai sesa nasa dosangki marhitehite mudarMi.

    3.O Jesus na marasiroha di saluhut pardosa i,Ondihon hami ai naeng jora, do hami mangulahon i,Sai sesa dosanami i marhitehite mudarMi.

    4.Sai lehon ma tu tondinami, tondiM, na sun badia i.Ibana mangajari hami, mangaradoti patikMi.Parbadiai ma hami be marhitehite mudarMi.

    5.Pasaor ma hami tu punguan ni angka na badia i,Na sonang di banuaginjang, mamuji-muji goarMi.Pamasuk hami be tusi marhite-hite mudarMi.

    Lagu: Julius Schröder, 1850
