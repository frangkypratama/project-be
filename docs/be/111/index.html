    111

    Patimbul be ma sangap

    BE 111Logu No. 129do = FBirama 3/4Lagu: Nikolaus Decius, 1522

    Memuat not angka dan not balok…

    Ende

    1.Patimbul be ma sangap ni Jahowa, DebatantaDok ma mauliatei tu Debata, Amanta.Ai pardangolan i sude, nang saluhutna jorbut peDialo Debatanta.

    2.Tongtong sombaonnami Ho mamuji salelengna,Dibaen na digomgomi Ho, HuriaMu sudena.Na so tardodo gogoMi, sai ingkon saut do rohaMi;On pe martua hami.

    3.O Jesus, na sasada Ho do anak ni Debata,Naung mago dipangolu Ho, diontok Ho do bada.O Jesus, Tuhannami on tangihon sombanami on,Asi rohaM di hami !

    4.O Tondi Parbadia i, ramoti ma sudena,Naung dipalua Kristus i, marhite na tinaonna.Ambati ma sibolis i, padao nang nasa jea i,Papos ma rohanami !

    Lagu: Nikolaus Decius, 1522
