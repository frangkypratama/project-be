> Na-ta-ma do i-ngo-ton-ta ta-on pa-ra-si-ni ro-ha-an on Ju-bi-le-um par-ni-ngo-tan, di-hi-ta sa-hu-ri-a on Nu-nga _ li-ma pu-lu ta-on naung _ o-jak Hu-ri-a on. Ta-on pa-ro-lo-po-lo-pon do on di na-sa Hu-ri-a on.
#V
Natama do ingotonta taon parasinirohaan on
Jubileum parningotan, dihita sahuria on
*Nunga limapulu taon naung ojak Huria on.
Taon parolopolopon do on di nasa Huria on.
-
Ta olophon denggan basa na ita jalo sian Debata,
saluhut do disarihon, na ringkot dingolunta on,
lam togu ma parsaoran dihita HuriaNa on.
radot masitangiangan ma, masiurupn hita on.
-
Ruas angka na matua, baen tiruan hita on tongtong,
maradophon anakhonta, tangkas ma ingot i tongtong,
sundut na umposo i do na sumingkati hita on,
togu-togu, tangiangkom ma, asa jonok tu Debata.
-
Rade haporseaonta lao mangadopi hamajuon on.
asa gabe pasupasu, panghorhon nii tu hita on,
tung tangkas ma tapoda i, anakhon ni Huria i,
asa jolma halomoan i di hita nang di Tuhan i.
