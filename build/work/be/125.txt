#V
Marlasniroha hita on, mamuji Debata:
Ai asi ni rohana i do bongot tu Tuhanta i,
Umbaen nuaeng mardomu i dison sadari on. :,: Dison sadari on.
-
Dibahen i manggogo be ma hita on sude.
Mamuji Tuhan Jesus i, parasiroha godang i,
Sibaen las ni rohanta i, :,: Nuaeng nang sogot pe. :,:
-
Alai taingot ma huhut mambaen sangap tongtong
Barita ni Tuhanta i marhite parangenta i
Di jolo ni donganta i, :,: Diatas tano on.
-
O, Jesus, tatap hami on, sai pargogoi sude
Marhitehite tondiMi pasauthon angka patikMi,
Mambaen rarat haritaMi :,: Di nasa jolma pe.
