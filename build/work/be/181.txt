> Ndang-na tar-pa-bo-a a-si ro-ha-ni De-ba-ta Ja-ho-wa mi-da jol-ma i; So tar-tim-bang i-ba do-sa i su-de, na bi-na-hen ni-ba ga-nup a-ri pe.
#V
Ndangna tarpaboa asi rohani
Debata Jahowa mida jolma i;
So tartimbang iba dosa i sude,
Na binahen niba ganup ari pe.
-
Sai husomba hami Ho, ale Tuhan:
Sesa dosanami sai asi rohaM!
Mombun baen rimasMu mida hami on,
Huihuthon hami Ho tu jolo on.
-
Sai dongani hami na mardagang i;
Roha nang pamatang sai ramoti i
Ho do Amanami na sumurung i,
Pangalualuan salelengna i.
