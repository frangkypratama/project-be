> Nun-ga ro di par-gu-ru-an ha-mi on na di-son, ma-si-ha-ngo-lu-an, Je-sus, sai hu-i-ngot ha-mi ha-ta-Mi, ni-dok-Mi: Ho naeng do-ngan na-mi,
#V
Nunga ro di parguruan hami on na dison,
Masihangoluan, Jesus, sai huingot hami
HataMi, nidokMi: Ho naeng dongan nami,
-
Baen ture pingkirannami, unang lao tu na dao,
mampar rohanami. Sai patinggil pinggolnami
Tu hataM, asa lam huantusi hami.
-
Suru Tondi parbadia baen ture hami be,
Ringgas jala ria asa bali haliluan,
Jea pe sasude, timbul hangoluan.
-
Pabotohon ma tu hami holong ni rohaMi
na pangolu hami. Asa lam mardosniroha hami on,
Sai tongtong mangalo na roa
-
Jesus, ndang diparsineang rohaMi hata ni
Na ro martangiang, i do pos ni rohanami
Mida Ho na dung ro paluahon hami
