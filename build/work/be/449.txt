> Sai sol-hot tu si-lang-Mi, Je-sus i-nga-nan-ku mu-al na ma-baor di-si, i ma i-nu-mon-ku si-lang-Mi, Tu-han-ki i ma pu-ji-on-ku, pai-ma so-got sa-hat au, i en-de-ho-non-ku,
#V
Sai solhot tu silangMi, Jesus ingananku
Mual na mabaor disi, i ma inumonku
SilangMi, Tuhanki i ma pujionku,
Paima sogot sahat au, i endehononku,
-
Lao ma au tu silangMi, i haporusanku.
Sai asi ma rohaMi unang tolak ahu, SilangMi....
-
Domu ma tu silangMi, i ma tioponku,
Asa unang lilu au, Ho ihuthononku, SilangMi.....
-
Laos maniop silangMi sai paimaonku
GokkonMi tu surgo i, tu siteanonku, SilangMi.....
