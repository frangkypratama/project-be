Sai ajari ma tongtong hami on huhut paria,
Martangiang tu Tuhanta, asa tongtong oloanNa
-

Pargogoi ma hami be maralohon dosanami,
Asa tung malua hami sian hadosaonnami,
-
Jolma manisia pe sai sondangi ma luhutna.
