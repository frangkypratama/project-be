Sai paherbang ma habongmu angka pardisurgo i,
baritahon nunga tubu Sipalua jolma i,
Ref.: Beta hita lao manomba Raja Sipalua i.

nunga dame hita jolma saor dohot Debata

boan pelean na arga, tu Raja na sorang i.

Debatanta gabe jolma, tuat tu portibion.
