> O Tu-han Je-sus, Ho Ra-jang-ku, ta-ngi-hon au, na-po-so-Mon! Naeng to-po-tan-ku do do-sang-ku tu Ho, Si-gom-gom ta-no on. Ai naeng ma-ngo-lu ton-ding-ki mar-hi-te-hi-te mu-dar-Mi.
#V
O Tuhan Jesus, Ho Rajangku, tangihon au, naposoMon!
Naeng topotanku do dosangku tu Ho, Sigomgom tano on.
Ai naeng mangolu tondingki marhitehite mudarMi.
-
Godang do dosa ni tanganku, rohangku pe gok dosa do,
Nang mata pe, nang pamatangku, luhutna i gok dosa do.
Sai sesa nasa dosangki marhitehite mudarMi.
-
O Jesus na marasiroha di saluhut pardosa i,
Ondihon hami ai naeng jora, do hami mangulahon i,
Sai sesa dosanami i marhitehite mudarMi.
-
Sai lehon ma tu tondinami, tondiM, na sun badia i.
Ibana mangajari hami, mangaradoti patikMi.
Parbadiai ma hami be marhitehite mudarMi.
-
Pasaor ma hami tu punguan ni angka na badia i,
Na sonang di banuaginjang, mamuji-muji goarMi.
Pamasuk hami be tusi marhite-hite mudarMi.
