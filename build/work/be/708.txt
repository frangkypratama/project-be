Jesus Ho do Sipalua i
Ref. : Jesus Tuhanki pangurupi i, holan Ho do haporusanki.
Jesus Tuhanki partahananki, holan Ho do Sipalua i.
Tung torop musu manosak au jala
ndang adong na mangurupi au.
O, ale Jesus sai tangihon au
holan Ho do Sipalua au.
