> Ho o Tu-han, ha-ho-lo-ngan ni ro-hang-ku sai tong-tong, sai ta-ngi-hon pa-ngi-do-an, sai u-ru-pi ma au on.
#V
Ho o Tuhan, haholongan ni rohangku sai tongtong,
Sai tangihon pangidoan, sai urupi ma au on.
-
Pasupasu ulaonku, tumpak ma sudena i,
Sai padenggan ma rohangku maradophon donganki.
-
Sai patiur ma rohangku mamingkiri basaMi,
Sesa ma sude dosangku, paimbaru todingki.
-
Iringiring au Tuhanku, sai tiopi tanganki,
Molo tos muse hosangku, abing au tu lambungMi.
