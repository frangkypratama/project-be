> Pa-tik na-im-ba-ru hu-le-hon, tu ha-mu a-sa ma-si-ha-ho-lo-ngan, ha-mu sa-ma ha-mu, so-ngon ho-long-ni ro-hang-ku ma-ra-dop-hon ha-mu, Si-an on do di tan-da ha-lak ha-mu si-se-an-Hu mo-lo ha-mu ma-si-ha-ho-lo-ngan, si-an on do di tan-da ha-lak ha-mu si-se-an-Hu mo-lo ha-mu ma-si-ha-ho-lo-ngan.
#V
Patik na imbaru hulehon, tu hamu asa masihaholongan,
hamu sama hamu, songon holongni rohangku maradophon hamu,
Sian on do di tanda halak hamu siseanHu
molo hamu masihaholongan,
sian on do di tanda halak hamu siseanHu
molo hamu masihaholongan.
