> A-le ton-ding-ku, naung he-he Tu-han-mu, Nu-nga di-ta-han do ba-nu-a to-ru, las ma ro-ham, ai mar-tu-a ma ho: Rap do-hot Je-sus ma-ngo-lu ma ho! Rap do-hot Je-sus ma-ngo-lu ma ho!
#V
Ale tondingku, naung hehe Tuhanmu, nunga ditaban do banua toru.
Las ma roham, ai martua ma ho:
:,: Rap dohot Jesus mangolu ma ho! :,:
-
Talu Sibolis dibahen Tuhanta, nang hamatean,
Nang dosa sudena. Sai dihalashon rohanta ma i.
:,: PangoluonNa do hita sude! :,:
-
Tahaburjuhon mangalo dosanta, jala tabaen Tuhan Jesus donganta.
Pos ma rohanta di Tuhanta i;
:,: PamonangonNa do hita sude! :,:
