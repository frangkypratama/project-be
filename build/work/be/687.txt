> Si-lang-Mi O Tu-han par-da-me-an-ki. par-si-gan-tu-ngan-ku ha-ngo-lu-an-ki, som-bu do u-as hu-sa-bam ro-hang-ki mu-dar-Mi O Je-sus ha-so-na-ngan-ki.
#V
SilangMi O Tuhan pardameanki.
parsigantunganku hangoluanki,
sombu do uas husabam rohangki
mudarMi O Jesus hasonanganki.
-
SilangMi O Tuhan pardameanki,
sesa do dosangku dibaen holongMi,
talu do musungku tanggal rante i,
hamamateMi do hamonanganki.
-
Sai haholonganhu Ho O Tuhanki,
ndang tadinghononhu salelengna i,
boan ma tondingku tu na sonang i,
huta sambulonku salelengna i.
