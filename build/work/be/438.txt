> Be-ta, sai ta-en-de-hon ma Tu-han-ta Je-sus i. Ho-sa-na di-U-se-hon do mang-kop-hop jol-ma i. Mu-dar ni Tu-han i do ha-ngo-lu-an-ki. Tung da-me ja-la las do ro-hang-ki, Tung mu-dar ni Tu-han-ku do ma-mu-ri ro-hang-ki, mar-o-lop-o-lop ton-ding-ki mar-ni-ngot a-si-Mi. O ha-tu-a-on i, na so boi sal-pu i! Beta, sai taendehon ma Tuhanta Jesus i.
Hosana diUsehon do mangkophop jolma i.
Mudar ni Tuhan i do hangoluanki. Tung dame jala las do rohangki,
Tung mudar ni Tuhanku do mamuri rohangki, marolop-olop tondingki marningot asiMi.
O hatuaon i, na so boi salpu i! Humophop au do mate Tuhanki
