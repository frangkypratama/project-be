Huhalashon, huringkoti haroroM tu tano on;
Jesus; Sipalua hami, jolma na mardangol on.
Ai marhite mudarMi Dihophopi Ho do i;
HuriaM asa badia Jala dohot marmulia.
-
Sai na di joloM do jongjong, huriaM na lea i,
Ai ringkot dihaalungunhon naeng tu Ho, Rajana i.
Dipaimaima Ho jala tung marhobas do,
Mangihuthon Ho, Parmonang, toguonMu tu na sonang.
-
Ro, sai ro Ho, o Tuhanku I! Jesus ro ma Ho tibu!
I panjou ni oroanMu, tatap haporsuhonki
Ro ma Ho, o Jesuski, tatap haporsuhonki
Sai tu hasampuran rea, alaponMu na porsea.
