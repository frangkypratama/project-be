> Lam ho-long ro-hang-ki tu Tu-han-ki, i-ma ta-ngi ang-ki o-loi ma i, lam ho-long ro-hang-ki tu Ho O Je-sus-ki o-loi ma i, O, Tu-han-ki
#V
Lam holong rohangki tu Tuhanki,
ima tangiangki oloi ma i,
lam holong rohangki tu Ho O Jesuski
oloi ma i, O, Tuhanki
-
Roha portibion hian di au,
nuaeng tu Tuhanki, manghirim au.
lam holong rohangki, sai i ma padanki
oloi ma i O Tuhanki.
-
Dung jumpang ajalhi, borhat ma au
sogot di surgo i sonang ma au,
disi pujionki Ho salelengna i,
oloi ma i O, Tuhnaki.
