> Ndang sa-di-ha-ri i di-dok ho, ho-ras par jo-lo, par-pu-di ro. Bi-lang ma a-ri-mi, ti-bu do sal-pu-i, a-tik so sa-nga be, han-sit-na i.
#V
Ndang sadihari i didok ho, horas parjolo, parpudi ro.
Bilang ma arimi, tibu do salpu i,
Atik so sanga be, hansitna i.
-
Ndang sadihari, alang dope, sai ro do ahu musemuse.
Sai pinaingot ho, sai tong ditoishon ho.
Tu dia nama ho? Lao mago do!
-
Manjou do Jesus sadari on, Ho naeng ditopot nuaeng tongon.
Sai jangkon Jesus i, silehon tua i,
Holong rohaNa i, sai golom i!
-
Roham pauba, lam, lam bot ne. Lam toras daging, ajal talpe.
Tupa so sahat ho, sai naeng tu surgo ho.
Elehelekku i, oloi ma i!
