> Ha-bot pe ro-ham, Por-suk jam-bar-mi, A-dong do pa-nga-pul, Tu-han-ta do i. I-ba-na do-ngan-mu ma-mor-san go-go, sai u-nang ho gang-gu, sai mo-nang ma ho.
#V
Habot pe roham, porsuk jambarmi,
Adong do pangapul, Tuhanta do i.
Ibana donganmu mamorsan gogo,
Sai unang ho ganggu, sai monang ma ho.
-
Saleleng dison dokdok do roham, hansit pamatangmu,
Marilu matam; Sai bereng tu ginjang, ai Jesus naeng ro!
Sai taon ma satongkin, mangolu ma ho!
-
Dung sahat tondim tu Tuhanta i, tarapul rohamu
Di lambungNa i, mansohot arsakmu,
Sai sonang ma ho, sai unang ho ganggu, martua ma ho!
