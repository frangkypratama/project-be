#V
O ulubalang na gogo, songon sadari on do Ho manaek tu hasangapon!
Naung sidung do ulaonMi; na ringkot patupaonMi, humophop hajolmaon.
Sabam, tongan do Ho muli tu na uli
Marhabangsa disiamun ni Jahowa.
-
Marloksaloksa Serapim, mamiburju Kerubim
Mamuji Ho na monang dibaen naung pinadomuMi
Saluhut manusia i tu Debata di ginjang.
Puji, Puji. Jesus Kristus naung patulus saluhutna
Timonahon ni AmaNa.
-
O Jesus, Ho do ulu ni luhutna na porsea i;
Ruasmu anggo hami, Ho hangoluannami on,
Ho hasonangannami on, Ho hagogoonnami!
Suru, togu, rohanami asa hami mangalehon
Tu Ho nasa hasangapon.
-
Sai taiti rohanami on, urupi hami on tongtong
Mangalo dosanami, paias rohanami be,
Parangenami baen ture, nang pangkataionnami.
Tompa roha, na imbaru, asa haru monang hami
Maralohon dosanami.
-
Pabulus langkanami be, patigor dalannami pe,
Lao tu banuaginjang. O Jesus, tatap hami on,
Na so tarbaen di tano on, Mardame manang sonang
Sonang, monang, di lambungMu bahenonMu hami sogot,
Dung tu surgo hami bongot.
